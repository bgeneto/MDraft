== The gauge

The gauge is a pressure sensor mounted below the low-water mark. It samples every
six minutes and reports the raw pressure, the water temperature and the
conductivity of the water above the sensor.

NOTE: A reading is only as good as its datum. Check the benchmark against the
brass bolt on the north pier before recording a new series.

[options="header"]
|===
| Channel | Range | Resolution
| Pressure | 0–10 bar | 0.01 mbar
| Temperature | −5–40 °C | 0.1 °C
| Conductivity | 0–60 mS/cm | 0.1 mS/cm
|===

== Calibration

The height of the surface above the survey datum follows the pressure:

[latexmath]
++++
h = h_0 + \frac{p - p_0}{\rho g}
++++

where latexmath:[h_0] is the datum, latexmath:[p_0] the atmospheric pressure,
latexmath:[\rho] the density of the water and latexmath:[g] the local
acceleration due to gravity.

[source,python]
----
def surface_height(pressure, datum, rho=1025.0, g=9.81):
    """Convert gauge pressure to height above the survey datum."""
    return datum + pressure / (rho * g)
----

TIP: Log the atmospheric pressure at the same time as the gauge reading. A
change in weather is easily mistaken for a change in the tide.

== Deployment

. Slack the mooring line until the sensor clears the low-water mark.
. Lower the sensor at least one metre below the lowest expected surface.
. Record the time, the datum and the atmospheric pressure in the deployment log.

WARNING: Do not lift the sensor by its cable. The cable carries the signal, not
the weight of the housing.
