            Welcome to TeXpresso
            
        
            A LaTeX editor that compiles as you type, in your browser.
            
    
              
      
            • The page follows your typing. No compile button: each change shows at once,
        and a click on the page takes you to its line.
              • 
      
            • Your documents stay with you. They are kept in this browser and work offline;
        they leave it only when you share or sync them.
              • 
      
            • Write together. Share a link, and edit the same document with others, live.
              • 
    
            
    
            How would you like to start?
            
    
            
      
      
      
    
            
    
            TeXpresso is in beta: tell us what works and what does not with Send feedback.
      The manual is behind the ? at the top right.
            
  
            
            

  
            
    
            Projects
            
      
            
    
            Kept in this browser.
            
    
              
    
              
  
              
              

  
              
    
              
  
              A file of the TeX distribution, read only.
              
  
              
              

  
              
    
              Settings
              
    
              Kept in this browser.
              
    
              
      This project
      
      
              
      
      
              
    
              
    
              
      Editor and preview
      
      
              The preview scrolls to keep it in view.
              
      
              
        
        
              Before the engine catches up, without kerning.
              
      
              
      
      
      
              In the editor: Esc for normal mode, :w saves.
              
    
              
    
              
      Theme
      
              
        
        
        
      
              
      
      
              Dark, the pages take the colors of the theme, their colors inverted (not those of
        the images). The PDF keeps those of the document.
              
    
              
    
              
      Spelling
      
      
      
              In the languages of the browser's settings.
              
      
      
              With its dictionaries, in the languages chosen below; right-click a word for suggestions.
              
      
              
        
        
              
      
              
      
              Only the text of the sources is checked: not commands, math, labels, references...
              
    
              
    
              
      AI assistance
      
      
              Answers in the AI tab of the conversation panel, from a model of the provider of
        your choice, with your API key. What you ask, and the files it reads, go from this browser to that provider,
        under its terms; never through TeXpresso.
              
      
              
        
        
              
          
          
          
              The endpoint's base: requests go to …/messages, …/chat/completions, …/models.
              
        
              
        
        
              
        
        
              Else it is forgotten when the tab closes.
              
        
        
        
              
          
              
      
              
    
              
    
              
      Performance and privacy
      
      
              Only the pages on screen are compiled; the next ones as you scroll to them. Saves battery on
        long documents.
              
      
      
              When the engine or the app fails: the error, the app's version and the browser,
        never your documents. Repeats are sent once a day.

              Please update your browser
              
    
              This version cannot run TeX: the engine needs WebAssembly's JavaScript Promise
      Integration, in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave from version 137, Firefox from 153 and Safari from 27
      (on iPhone and iPad, with iOS 27). Update your browser, or open the app in another one, to
      compile; meanwhile your projects can still be edited.

      with the File System Access API.

                Connect to GitHub
                
    
                TeXpresso reaches GitHub with a personal access token, kept in this browser only.
      Make one on GitHub
      (a fine-grained token):
                
    
                  
      
                1. Repository access: the repositories of your projects, or all of them.
                  2. 
      
                2. Permissions, for repositories: add Contents, set to
        Read and write. It is the one TeXpresso needs, to read the files of a repository
        (Pull) and to write them (Commit); a new token does not have it until you add it.
                  3. 
      
                3. Generate token, and paste it here.
                  4.
